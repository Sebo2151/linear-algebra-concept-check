What this tool is for

Introductory linear algebra can feel like a collection of computational procedures. This tool emphasizes a different habit: read a mathematical claim carefully, identify the relevant definition or theorem, and decide whether the claim really follows.

The app deliberately uses mostly straightforward conceptual questions, with a smaller number of questions that test awareness of missing hypotheses. Explanations and counterexamples are part of the practice, not just answer keys.

It remembers broad areas of difficulty in your browser and gently favors those ideas in later practice. It is not intended to replace a full spaced-repetition system.
